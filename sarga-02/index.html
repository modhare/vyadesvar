Sarga 2 — Kṣetranirmāṇaḥद्वितीयः सर्गः · क्षेत्रनिर्माणः — परशुरामाच्या भूमीची (रामक्षेत्राची) निर्मिती

द्वितीयः सर्गः · क्षेत्रनिर्माणः

The making of Paraśurāma's land (Rāmakṣetra)परशुरामाच्या भूमीची (रामक्षेत्राची) निर्मिती · 60 verses60 श्लोक

Paraśurāma calms down and lectures the sea in nīti maxims: poison needs no testing, insulting the great is like throwing dry grass on fire, the thoughtless sink like a cow in mud, the bending reed survives the flood (2.1–11). Since his arrow can never be wasted, he will shoot it only 5–6 yojanas, and the sea must withdraw to that point (12–17). That night termites, repaying an old debt to the sea, try to gnaw his bowstring. He curses them never to live on rock and always to have earth in their mouths, and the sea consoles them with water (18–28), an etiological tale for termite mud. At dawn the arrow flies, the sea recedes and the new land appears (29–43). Paraśurāma enters it, and the poet gives its extent, boundaries, holiness and names (44–60).
