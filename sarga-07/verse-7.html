Verse 7श्लोक 7

Sarga 7 — Kṛṣṇāvatāravarṇanaḥ · सप्तमः सर्गः · कृष्णावतारवर्णनः

Chandas · Anuṣṭubh
अरिसीरायुधौ साम्बुनिरम्बुजलदप्रभौ ।
वन्दे कृष्णबलौ देवौ देवकीरोहिणीसुतौ ।।७।।
arisīrāyudhau sāmbunirambujaladaprabhau । vande kṛṣṇabalau devau devakīrohiṇīsutau
सन्धिविग्रह · sandhi-vigraha (word by word)
अरि-सीर-आयुधौ । साम्बु-निरम्बु-जलद-प्रभौ । वन्दे । कृष्ण-बलौ । देवौ । देवकी-रोहिणी-सुतौ ।
ari-sīra-āyudhau । sāmbu-nirambu-jalada-prabhau । vande । kṛṣṇa-balau । devau । devakī-rohiṇī-sutau ।
संधी · sandhi notes
सीर + आयुधौ = सीरायुधौ (सवर्णदीर्घ) · स + अम्बु = साम्बु · निः + अम्बु = निरम्बु (विसर्ग → र्)
अन्वयः · anvaya (prose word order)
अरिसीरायुधौ साम्बुनिरम्बुजलदप्रभौ देवकीरोहिणीसुतौ कृष्णबलौ देवौ वन्दे ।
English

I salute the two gods, Kṛṣṇa and Bala(rāma) — the sons of Devakī and Rohiṇī — whose weapons are the discus and the plough, who shine like clouds laden with water and clouds without water.

मराठी

चक्र व नांगर ही आयुधे धारण करणाऱ्या, जलयुक्त व जलहीन मेघांप्रमाणे कांती (शोभा) असणाऱ्या त्या देवकी व रोहिणी यांच्या पुत्रांना, कृष्ण व बलराम या दोघा देवांना मी वन्दन करतो.

Textual note. Verse 7.4, line 2: the book's गिरिराट् भाराक्रान्तो → गिरिराड्भाराक्रान्तो (sandhi applied; spelling only). Verse 7.12, line 1: the book's बक्यसुनिवृत्तिकारि → बक्यसुनिवृत्तिरकारि (निवृत्तिः + अकारि; restores the missing syllable of वसन्ततिलका and the construction with येन). Verse 7.7, Marathi: the book's क्रांती → कांती ("radiance"). Verse 7.13, line 3: the book's स्वयं चे → स्वयं च ("and himself"; the 5th syllable must be light). Verse 7.16, line 1: the book's स सप्तहायनवया → यः सप्तहायनवया (conjectural). The first syllable must be heavy. सः cannot supply it: the visarga of सः is dropped before a consonant (एतत्तदोः सुलोपः), leaving a light स. यः keeps its visarga, scans, and gives the relative clause that तं गोपतिं… प्रपद्ये answers, as in 7.13, 7.15 and 7.19. Verse 7.17, line 1: the book's याऽरिष्ट → योऽरिष्ट. An avagraha after या is impossible in sandhi (या + अ would merge to यारि-); योऽ comes from यः + अरिष्ट, and the masculine relative यः is needed for Kṛṣṇa, answered by स हन्तु in line 4. The meter is the same either way. Verse 7.17: line 1, the book's …केशिकं स → …केशिकंस- (केशि + कंस, "Keśin, Kaṃsa", inside the long compound ending in दैत्यान्; meter unchanged; a separate केशिकं would be an odd accusative singular among plurals, and a separate स would duplicate स हन्तु in line 4; the book's Marathi reads स as सह and so leaves Kaṃsa out). Line 2, चाणूरासभ… left as printed at the reader's request, though as printed it has 13 syllables, one short of वसन्ततिलका, with a heavy 3rd syllable where a light one is needed; चाणूर + रासभ would restore both, and the book's own Marathi lists the ass-demon separately (गर्दभाचे रूप घेतलेला राक्षस). Open for review. Verse 7.19, line 2: my misreading परयाच्च corrected to the book's पररयाच्च ("from the onrush of enemies"; scans). Verse 7.19, line 1: दुःपर्षदो left as printed (the strict Pāṇinian sandhi would be दुष्पर्षदो; spelling only). Verse 7.14, Marathi: the book's ताबडबोत → ताबडतोब. Verse 7.20, Marathi: continues on page 62. Verse 7.21, line 2: the book's सकालन् → सकलान् ("all", Marathi सर्व). The 7th syllable must be light (स-क-, not स-का-), and the accusative plural ending -आन् is needed to agree with दैत्यान्. Verse 7.27, line 4: the book's पङ्ककक्षाश्रयाशम् → पङ्कजाक्षाश्रयाशम् (पङ्कजाक्ष, "lotus-eyed", Marathi कमलनेत्रांच्या; same syllable weights, so meter unchanged). Verse 7.28, Marathi: completed from page 64.

Spiritual meaningआध्यात्मिक अर्थ

English

The twin veneration holds jñāna and bala together: the dark rain-cloud (sāmbu) is Kṛṣṇa's compassion that pours like monsoon rain; the white, waterless autumn cloud (nirambu) is Balarāma's purity and strength that holds the sky steady. Cakra and hala — the weapon that cuts and the plough that tills — say the Lord both destroys adharma and cultivates dharma in the devotee's field. Naming both mothers, Devakī and Rohiṇī, the poet honours the two wombs of the avatāra and, by extension, all motherhood that bears the divine.

मराठी

दोघांचे एकत्र वंदन ज्ञान व बल यांना एकत्र धरते : काळा पाण्याचा ढग (साम्बु) म्हणजे मान्सूनधारेसारखी वर्षावणारी कृष्णाची करुणा; पाण्याविरहित पांढरा शरद्-मेघ (निरम्बु) म्हणजे बलरामाची शुद्धता व आकाश स्थिर धरणारे बल. चक्र व नांगर — कापणारे शस्त्र व नांगरणारे हत्यार — सांगतात की भगवान अधर्माचा छेदही करतो व भक्ताच्या क्षेत्रात धर्माची नांगरणीही करतो. दोन्ही माता, देवकी व रोहिणी यांची नावे घेऊन कवी अवताराच्या दोन्ही गर्भांना व त्याद्वारे दिव्याला धारण करणाऱ्या सर्व मातृत्वाला मान देतो.

Commentariesटीका

Traditional
English

Iconographically the verse is exact: Kṛṣṇa's weapon is the discus (cakra; ari here, 'the enemy (of demons)' or the wheel's rim-edge), Balarāma's is the plough (hala/sīra) — the farmer-god who drags the Yamunā with it in the Bhāgavata. The cloud simile is classical stock for the brothers' contrasted complexions: Kṛṣṇa dark as the monsoon cloud (Kālidāsa's Meghadūta opens by likening the cloud to Kṛṣṇa), Balarāma fair as the autumn cloud drained of rain. Devakī-Rohiṇī-sutau states the Bhāgavata's birth account precisely — Kṛṣṇa of Devakī, Balarāma (Saṃkarṣaṇa) transferred to Rohiṇī's womb.

मराठी

मूर्तिशास्त्राच्या दृष्टीने श्लोक तंतोतंत अचूक आहे : कृष्णाचे आयुध चक्र (येथील 'अरि' — दैत्यांचा शत्रू किंवा चक्राची धार), बलरामाचे नांगर (हल/सीर) — भागवतात त्यानेच यमुना ओढलेला शेतकरी-देव. मेघोपमा बंधूंच्या विरुद्ध वर्णांसाठी शास्त्रीय रूढी आहे : कृष्ण मान्सूनधारेसारखा काळा (कालिदासाचा मेघदूत मेघाला कृष्णाशी तुलना करूनच सुरू होतो), बलराम पाण्याविरहित शरद्-मेघासारखा गौर. 'देवकी-रोहिणी-सुतौ' भागवताचे जन्मवृत्त तंतोतंत सांगते — देवकीपासून कृष्ण, रोहिणीच्या गर्भात संक्रमित झालेला बलराम (संकर्षण).

Editor's note
English

Metre: Anuṣṭubh; the whole verse is built of duals (dvandvas and dual adjectives), saluting the pair as one unit. Note the edition's textual record for this sarga: in v. 7's Marathi the book's 'krāntī' is corrected to 'kāntī' ('radiance', not 'revolution'), and the printed sandhi girirāṭ bhārākrāntaḥ (v. 7.4) is normalised to girirāḍbhārākrāntaḥ — spelling only. The sāmbu-nirambu antithesis ('with-water, without-water') is a prized yamaka-like echo the poet sustains across the line.

मराठी

वृत्त : अनुष्टुभ्; संपूर्ण श्लोक द्विवचनांनी घडला आहे (द्वंद्व व द्विवचनी विशेषणे) — दोघांचे एकत्र वंदन. सर्ग ७-च्या पाठभेद-नोंदीत नोंद : श्लोक ७-च्या मराठीत ग्रंथाचे 'क्रांती' ते 'कांती' ('तेज', 'क्रांती' नव्हे) असे शुद्ध केले आहे. 'साम्बु-निरम्बु' (पाण्यासह-पाण्यरहित) हा विरुद्धाभास कवीने संपूर्ण ओळीत यमकासदृश नादाने टिकवून धरला आहे.
