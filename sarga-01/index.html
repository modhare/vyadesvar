Sarga 1 — Rāmasāgarasamāgamaḥप्रथमः सर्गः · रामसागरसमागमः — परशुरामाची सागराशी भेट

प्रथमः सर्गः · रामसागरसमागमः

Paraśurāma's meeting with the Oceanपरशुरामाची सागराशी भेट · 46 verses46 श्लोक

Maṅgalācaraṇa to Gaṇeśa, Vāgīśvarī and Kṛṣṇa (1–3). Praise of Guhāgar village and its agrahāra on the sea coast, and of the god Vyāḍeśvara in its temple (4–6), followed by the poet's pledge to tell the māhātmya of the god and the village (7). Paraśurāma's birth and deeds: the 21 destructions of the kṣatriyas, the Samantapañcaka lakes, and the gift of the whole earth to Kaśyapa (8–17). Unwilling to live on land he has given away, he leaves Mount Mahendra, reaches the Sahyādri, bathes at Mahābaleśvara and orders the sea to withdraw (18–30). The sea ignores him, and Paraśurāma's rage and arrow-fitting shake the universe (31–38). The sea comes in brāhmaṇa form, sings his praise and surrenders (39–46).
