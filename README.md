# vyadesvar
Repository for श्रीव्याडेश्वरोदय or श्रीव्याडेश्वरमहाकाव्यम् also known as Vyāḍeśvara Mahatmya.

The श्रीव्याडेश्वरोदय ("the rise of Śrī Vyāḍeśvara"), printed as श्रीव्याडेश्वरमहाकाव्यम् with a Marathi meaning for every verse, is a Sanskrit mahākāvya in fourteen sargas. It tells the sacred history of Guhagar on the Konkan coast and its presiding deity, Vyāḍeśvara (in the verses usually वाडेश्वर): how Paraśurāma won this land from the sea, how the sage Vyāḍi installed a liṅga here, how that liṅga was hidden in the Kali age and found again, how a king built its temple, and how a brahmin founded the village of Guhagar around it. It ends with hymns to the god.
