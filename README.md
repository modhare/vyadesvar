# श्रीव्याडेश्वरोदयम् — Vyadeshwar Mahatmya

A verse-by-verse Sanskrit deep-dive library for the **Śrī Vyāḍeśvarodayam**
("the rise of Śrī Vyāḍeśvara"), printed as श्रीव्याडेश्वरमहाकाव्यम् —
also known as the *Vyāḍeśvara Mahatmya*.

The kāvya, in fourteen sargas, tells the sacred history of Guhagar on the
Konkan coast and its presiding deity, Vyāḍeśvara (in the verses usually
वाडेश्वर): how Paraśurāma won this land from the sea, how the sage Vyāḍi
installed a liṅga here, how that liṅga was hidden in the Kali age and found
again, how a king built its temple, and how a brahmin founded the village
of Guhagar around it. It ends with hymns to the god.

## Browse the library

This is a **static, offline-first** library — no server, no build step, no
internet needed. Open `index.html` in any browser and start reading.

```
index.html                  ← library home
sarga-01/ … sarga-14/
    index.html              ← sarga overview
    verse-1.html … verse-N.html   ← one page per verse (694 total)
intro-verses/
    index.html              ← Sthaladarśanam: temple photos & maps
```

Every verse page carries: the Devanāgarī text with IAST, sandhi-vigraha
(padaccheda), anvaya, word-by-word grammar cards with usage examples,
English and Marathi translations, the spiritual meaning (EN + Marathi),
traditional and editorial commentaries, metre, and textual notes where the
manuscript, the print, and the metre disagree.

A language toggle (EN / EN + मराठी / मराठी) is on every page.

## Source, manuscript, scholarship

- **The poem**: *Śrī Vyāḍeśvarodayam* by Viśvanātha Hari Pitre (Kauśika
  gotra, Chitpāvan, lifelong resident of Guhagar) — son of Mahādeva,
  grandson of Hari, descended from Kāśīnātha. Composed, by the poet's own
  account, after long labour.
- **Date**: the poet's colophon (14.28) gives Śaka 1559 = **1637 CE** as a
  chronogram; Shastri's catalogue synopsizes it as 1628 AD. Both are
  recorded; the discrepancy is left open.
- **Manuscript**: the sole known copy is Asiatic Society, Kolkata,
  MS No. 5213 (43 folia, legible Nāgara, complete but fragile).
- **Scholarship**: noticed by P. K. Gode; studied by A. D. Pusalkar
  (JBBRAS Vol. XXVII; 1952 Abhinandan Grantha, pp. 83–102; text in
  *Oriental Thought*, Sec. 10).
- **This edition**: the Marathi book *Shri Vyadeshwar Mahatmya*, published
  by Raghunath Hari Apte (Dadar, Mumbai) at the prompting of Shri R.
  Tikekar; Marathi translation by Dr. M. D. Paradkar (completed
  22 September 1980). A devotional reading text, not a critical edition —
  the translator notes corruptions in his sources and frequent faults of
  metre in the original.
- **Spelling**: the colophons read व्याडेश्वर; the title page and many
  verses read वाडेश्वर (metre permitting). Both forms are kept as printed.

## Our intent

This work is an act of recollection and digitization: to preserve a
17th-century Sanskrit sthala-kāvya known from a single manuscript, and to
place it in the hands of every devotee of Vyāḍeśvara, every researcher,
and every lover of Sanskrit. What is gathered here is offered at the feet
of the Lord.
